Hannibal: file on you

Hannibal

A coach for your mind, not your moves

Three characters watch how you play on Lichess: when you rush, when you freeze, how you take a loss. After each game they tell you what they saw. They never judge the position or suggest a move.

1Connect Nemotron

Hannibal runs on NVIDIA Nemotron models hosted by Nebius. Paste a Token Factory key from tokenfactory.nebius.com. It stays in this browser.

2Let them read your games

An agent reads up to 60 of your recent games, with the clock time of every move, and investigates them with tools. A verifier recomputes every finding from the data and sends weak ones back.

    3Play

    Lichess bans extensions that help you play, so by default the characters only speak during games against the computer. Every game gets a debrief when it ends.
